The World of Gunpla

About the Hobby

Gunpla is the popular hobby of building plastic scale models of mecha from the Mobile Suit Gundam franchise. Manufactured by Bandai, these kits are famous for requiring no glue to assemble and coming molded in color, making them accessible to beginners and rewarding for expert customizers.

Model Kit Grades

Gunpla kits are categorized into different "grades" based on their scale, complexity, and level of detail. Here are the most common grades:

  • High Grade (HG): 1/144 scale. The most standard and collectible line with a vast lineup of mobile suits.
  • Real Grade (RG): 1/144 scale. Features an advanced inner frame and highly detailed color separation.
  • Master Grade (MG): 1/100 scale. Larger, more detailed kits with full mechanical inner frames.
  • Perfect Grade (PG): 1/60 scale. The ultimate Gunpla experience with massive size, intricate details, and often LED gimmicks.
